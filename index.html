Pix Store

loading… admin Pixlets for your Pixlet, a software platform for ESP32 devices.
On the device: tap Pix Store on the Pixlet home and pick the Pixlets you want; they download straight to the card. From here: download firmware.bin, then upload it with the SD Manager web page into /apps/<id>/ on the card together with an app.json (the button below copies it). The launcher lists the app on its next visit.
